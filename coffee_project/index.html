Our Story

Crafted with Passion

Founded in Phnom Penh, Brew & Bean started as a small roastery with a big dream — to bring world-class coffee to every cup. We source beans directly from farms in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Vietnam.

Every drink is prepared by trained baristas who care about the details: temperature, timing, and the perfect crema.
